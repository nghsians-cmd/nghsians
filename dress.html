01White collared shirt+

Equality, discipline and a neat appearance.

  • A shared standard. The same white shirt reduces visible differences in everyday clothing and keeps the focus on being students together.
  • Ready for school. A collar and a tidy fit give the outfit a formal appearance and a consistent daily routine. Shirts may have short or full sleeves.
  • Light colour. White reflects more sunlight than dark colours. Breathable fabric and a comfortable fit also matter in Chattogram’s heat.
02School badge+

Identity, belonging and school pride.

  • Recognisably NGHS. The school logo sits on the shirt’s left-chest pocket and identifies the school a student belongs to.
  • One community. A shared badge connects classmates, batches and the wider school community.
  • A visible reminder. It represents the school’s identity and the values students carry with them.
03Dark navy formal pants+

Practical, smart and consistent.

  • Everyday practicality. The dark colour makes minor dirt and marks less noticeable during the school day.
  • Clear contrast. Deep navy pairs with bright white for a neat, formal appearance.
  • A consistent fit. Full-length formal pants keep the lower half of the outfit uniform across students.
04White socks+

Cleanliness and a consistent finish.

  • Care is visible. White fabric shows dirt readily, making it easier to notice when socks need washing. Fresh, clean socks support daily hygiene.
  • One simple colour. Plain white avoids competing colours and patterns and gives everyone the same finishing detail.
  • Head-to-toe coordination. White socks connect the dark navy pants with the white shoes.
05White shoes+

Comfort, movement and a neat athletic appearance.

  • Made for movement. Comfortable, well-fitting white school shoes suit an active school day. Flexible canvas styles with suitable soles can support everyday movement and PT.
  • A coordinated line-up. Matching white footwear completes the uniform during classes, assembly and drills.
  • A tidy finish. Keeping shoes clean maintains the bright, consistent appearance of the outfit.
06Navy winter sweatshirt+

A warm layer that belongs with the uniform.

  • Colour continuity. Navy matches the dark navy formal pants, keeping the winter outfit visually consistent.
  • Over the shirt. The plain V-neck layer leaves the white collar visible and adds warmth in cooler weather.
  • Simple and coordinated. Ribbed cuffs and hem give the layer a neat finish. The shirt’s pocket badge is covered by the sweatshirt.
